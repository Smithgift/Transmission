## The Labyrinth

James initial urge was to charge down the doors into the inky blackness beyond, but he had discipline from months fighting with the Trogs. The scouts hurried inside, their torches showing the outlines of winding passages.

"Now we wait," Peter said.

James waited. One worldclimber had told him this was the worst part, since you could do nothing and there was always a chance of another monster attack while you were waiting for the scouts to be done. So you couldn't relax either.

One, two, three, four in. He held his breath. One, two, three, four out. However their bodies worked in this place, breathing exercises still calmed him.

Though inwardly, he was terrified.

But he still had to wait.

"Remember," Peter said. "If you see a spawner---they're like lamps in reverse---smash it first. Then the sub-Guardians. Then the Guardian. But whatever it takes, _survive!_"

"What happens if you get lost?" the older lady asked.

"You take a left turn every intersection and hope you find your way back to the marked path before something eats you. Now be quiet."

She shut up.

Scouts came back out, waving their hands in a _No_ sign. They must have only found dead ends.

James waited. He hated waiting. Stella had always been able to---

A scout party returned. "Found it!" they called.

The Salamander parties grouped up and followed them in. Now suddenly there was nothing between James's party and the mouth of the Labyrinth. The other parties, in a reasonable order, followed, and soon enough James himself stepped inside.

He could hear shouts and roars and skittering inside, but didn't see it as he followed down Peter down the narrow route marked by white chalk. The walls were sufficatingly close, and they passed through several rooms where piles of monster meat or human equipment showed causalties of this final battle.

James heard a close roar and reacted just in time to stop an enormous blood-red troll from caving his skull in with a spiked club. His spear through its neck solved that, but no time to stop, the next party was already pushing against him.

The sounds of skittering and battle grew louder and louder, until suddenly the passages got wider and wider, ending in a second set of imposing if smaller doors.

Behind was utter chaos.

Salamanders and earlier parties fought hordes of skeletal yellow scorpions so thick that they crawled over each other in the hopes of getting to their targets. From several small corridors scorpions spawned out of the spawners, anti-lamps that darkened rather than revealed. Some had already been smashed, but others still spat out more scorpions.

In the central dias, surrounded by four large red scorpions, was one titanic black scorpion with numerous stingers. Those stingers shot knives out that sliced through scorpion and human flesh alike.

"Get the spawners, then the healers, then the boss!" Peter shouted. They headed towards the nearest spawner, but the waves of scorpions were so thick James could barely keep his spear moving.

Idiot, he had scrolls! He tried to stop spearing to open his inventory, but the scorpions were so thick he couldn't get a chance. He tried reaching with his spear hand, but ended up clocking himself in the head. Scorpions took the chance and started crawling over him...

But Drake's party came crashing through and saved him. No time to say a word, and James opened his inventory, grabbed a scroll, and tore it in two. He felt power rush through him, and he pointed at the spawner. Walls of ice shot forth and froze the creatures. The older lady, screaming a battle cry, climbed over and stabbed her spear into the spawner, shattering it.

The other scorpions swarmed her, but nearby parties cleared them off. She was covered in glowing red mark. James ripped out a salve and rubbed it wherever he could. And popped a pill in his mouth while he was at it.

The battle seemed to be turning towards the Trogs with only one spawner left, but James didn't have much time to look around. They charged the spawner, but in the chaos James could only stab with his spear.

Heavy skittering came as one large red scorpions zoomed terrifyingly fast towards the mass of Trogs. It stung through several Trogs, each dying instantly. James barely stopped it by swinging his sword and slicing it off. Then Drake charged it. A wave of knives shot down and sliced him into red, then he flashed away in light.

James turned to see the black scorpion on the move. Some Trogs foolishly hammered it with scrolls, but red light shone from the three surviving red scorpions and healed it instantly.

A crash, and the last spawner was down. Now it was just a mix of Trogs and the surviving scorpions.

James charged the nearest red one, but it slammed its claw into him and he flew some distance before hitting a wall. He thought to pull out a salve and rub it on his body before it came charging again.

He got out another scroll and tore it. The ray of light slammed into the red scorpion and stunned in, then someone's dart slew it.

James got up and found himself unsteady. Then---wait, where was everyone?

The mass of Trogs had been reduced to isolated survivors. The Guardian scorpion itself was untouched, and one of the two remaining red scorpions was chasing down Trogs.

"Retreat!" a Tribe officer called.

James didn't want to, but at that point he knew the battle was hopeless, and the only remaining hope was to get out before the doors shut. He followed the officer down into the maze itself, only for a red troll to lumber out and slam its club into him. James swore and sliced it with his sword, but then heard skittering behind him.

He ran. He ran down the maze, trying to find the way out. _Please,_ he prayed, not knowing to whom. _Please let me---_

He saw the doors slowly swing closed. With every last bit of strength he had, he ran and leapt for the door---
