"Nurck," James said. "That doesn't say anything."

"It means that if we do something _without_ something we get cursed?" Stella asked.

"Maybe. Because _raui_ can be both venture, take or fight, depending on context."

"What's the curse of falling?" Violet asked.

"You fall up instead of down."

"It should be called levitation, then," James argued.

"I'm giving you the literal Oru," Lucky snapped.

Ralph shook his head. "Levitation isn't that bad of a curse, but who the nurck knows what will set it off? Do we try this one or look for another?"
