## Ancient Heroes

Massive pieces of ghostly green armor floated up from the ground and assembled themselves. Weapons followed, until faceless giants made of ethereal armor stood around them.

Lucky said something in Oru. The giants touched their right shoulder with their left hand. She said something else, pointed at the Eyedra, and they hurried off.

BOOM! A cannon fired. This ray hit a major eye, but the Eyedra was slower to regrow.

"What the nurck are those?" James demanded.

"That's what that scroll did," Lucky said. She opened up her stats screen and watched the almost-full magic bar slowly decrease. "Apparently 'let us recall the ancient heroes' means literally summon them."

"Violet!" Stella said. The ice was closing in on Violet's neck.

"If this is the end, it's the end," Violet said, almost philosophically.

"Hold on to that Phoenix Sword and it's not," Ralph thundered, suddenly angry. "James! Find something to hold her hand in place!"

James pulled off his bandana and wrapped it around Violet's hand, and Ralph knotted it in place.

Lucky chanted in Oru again, and another ghostly army assembled. With another Oru order, they charged the Eyedra.

BOOM! A cannon fired, and followed by another BOOOOOM as it exploded. The Eyedra hissed, but the hiss was weaker.

Stella helped Violet lay on the ground. Then she tried scraping the ice away with a knife, but it simply regrew.

Battle cries sounded, and James turned to see the guards leading a charge towards the Eyedra. James could just barely see the ghostly giants, but they were doing massive damage to the Eyedra. It swung out eyes like maces, smashing giants, but the movements were wide and sometimes missed, like it was drunk.

Ralph aimed his hand and shot another fire spear. It struck an already-reddened eye of the Eyedra, and then the red spread across its whole body. As the hissing trailed off, the red suddenly became solar-white, and with an enormous rumble, the Eyedra exploded into specks of light.

James screamed a war cry in triumph. "Take that you---!"

_"VIOLET!"_ Stella shrieked.
