## The Dream

The next world seemed to be made of brown tiles floating in... something. A something that was all around and above them, shimmering through every color in blobs of light.

The crew gathered together, looking at each other with uncertainty.

James laid the captain down. There was no hope he'd survive.

"Ma'am," the captain said, looking up at Lucky. "I tried so hard."

"Yes, you did," Lucky said calmly. "Look! You've saved most of your crew. And we're here in the fourth world."

"But... but I didn't save everyone." He started to weep. "Did I do a good enough job?"

"You did a great job," Lucky told him. "You did everything you had to do..."

"Can I go home... I'm so sorry. I should have known..."

Lucky bent over and whispered something into his ear. James caught the word 'Joshua.'

The captain nodded, and then disappeared in a flash, items and his torn uniform spilling out.

James realized the entire room was looking at Lucky. No one moved.

"We're here," Lucky said. "We're in the fourth world."




"Um, excuse me!" Stella said.

James saw what Stella had just seen. "What the _nurck?_"

Everyone looked, and the argument ended instantly.

The brown tiles leading from the old portal were crumbling and falling away, and, more concerningly, so was the tiles that made up the room itself. A fragment of the wall broke off and fell.

""




"Is there _anywhere_ safe?" Stella asked.

Lucky shrugged. "I don't know. All we Perfects know of the fourth world is that it's called the Dream."

"A nightmare, more like," James said.




"So, just saying," Lucky said. "If you and James need some private time, Violet and I could head over there for a bit..."

"Not that I would mind, but I'd prefer to engage in that kind of thing when the nurcking _floor_ isn't about to kill us all," James noted.

"Me, too," Stella said, and then blushed.

"I'm a bit more worried about a party coming in," Violet said. "Anyone who sees Lucky might attack all of us. At least the floor is predictable. Usually."
