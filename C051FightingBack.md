## Fighting back

The ground underneath quaked as the Eyedra entered Ironspire itself, demolishing the walls on its way to destroy the next turret holding the cannon. Lucky also shook, as if everything she had done was now forfeit for this failure.

Stella sat by her, and covered Lucky's palm with hers. Lucky took a breath, and shook a little less. "What now?"

"Plenty of room to escape now," James whispered.

BOOM! BOOM! The cannons fired. Another HISS rang out, and another crash.

"Cannons might kill it," Ralph said. "If they're not---"

The loudest explosion James had ever heard in his life rang out. He turned to see the Eyedra pull back a red-mangled eye, only for the whole eye to dissolve into worms, which sprouted more eyes.

"If they're not destroyed by the Eyedra first," Violet finished.

"They can't keep firing continuously," Stella said. "If their flux radiators overload, the cannons will either explode or melt."

BOOM! BOOM!

"How long?" James asked.

"I don't know, but I think they can't afford to care about maintenance life right now."

HISS! The Eyedra smashed a building into the ground.

"We don't have time to talk," Lucky said. "Let's run."

"No," James said.

"'No?'" Lucky asked, incredulously.

"I'm not letting it destroy all this city and kill all these people," James said.

"I agree," Ralph said.

"I don't," Violet said. "Don't you all know the first rule of survival in the Destination? Survival is more important than anything. I don't think we can possibly do enough damage if the cannons can't."

BOOM! James noticed one fewer cannon had fired.

"She's right," Lucky said. "No one here will die permanently. Nothing here can't be rebuilt. We have as much right to survive as anyone else."

James hesitated. Fleeing would be cowardice in a way, but maybe Lucky was right. After all, this was as transient as anything else in the Tower of Worlds. Did they really want to throw their "lives" away to spare anyone else's "lives", when everyone would inevitably respawn anyway?

"Lucky," Stella said. "Do you know your range with fire spears?"

"They keep going until they hit something, like all fire spears."

"Then let's get a clear angle at the Eyedra and hammer it on the way out. If we can kill it, we can, but we don't get anything if we die here. No one else does, either, because the Eyedra will just despawn if there's no one nearby to kill."

Ralph shook his head. "I hate to say it, but she has a point. A last stand means nothing to a Titan. Let's go."

They got up. "Where are you going?" a guard shouted.

"To fight!" James yelled, and they ran onwards. Now if only he could find another spear---he had his backup short sword, but against worms...

"Everyone, stop at that fire," Violet said with sudden authority.

"Why?" James asked, but they all stopped at the firepit, the fire merrily blazing as if reminding every Transmitted nearby that monsters could not spawn near fires. 

"If Lucky is radiating red, we should all be able to learn red spells," Violet said. "So let's do that real quick, and we can spare some of Lucky's magic meter."

"I don't know if it will work, but we should try." Lucky got out her white bowl. "Most fire and attack spells are red. So look for those."

James took the bowl, and Violet handed him a scroll. He put it in over the fire, and the scroll turned to ash. Then he hesitated.

"Draw a circle on the top of your left hand with the ash," Lucky said. "Quickly!"

James drew, and felt nothing different. But he opened up his magic screen to see a red bar awaiting his touch.

BOOM! As if belatedly arriving to the party, a single cannon fired.

James passed the bowl to Ralph. In under a minute they all had more spells. Stella was last and looked a little uncomfortable, but did it quickly anyway.

_BOOOOOM!_ A cannon exploded in the distance.

James aimed with his finger and pressed the button. A fire spear shot and hit an eye, leaving a mark. The whole eye dissolved and split into more eyes. "Let's aim for the eyes," he said.

Ralph fired another spear, and then the rest. "Careful!" Lucky called. "Don't fill your magic meter!"

The Eyedra HISSED, louder and angrier, as if finally sick of the humans who pestered it. One giant eye shot off, and came rolling down the ruined streets---towards them!

James held out his hand, but found no spear in it. Nurck! He had dropped it earlier. He drew his short sword, knowing that it could do little as the eye rolled towards them---Stella screaming---

And then the eye exploded. Worms spewed everywhere, a mass breaking off and chasing other Transmitted down another street. The other mass charged them, some in front, and one standing up in the back and hissing, _"Waru Aluru Oru Kara Oru Pari,"_

James felt as if someone had dumped a mattress full of ball bearing on him. He tried to stand, but was nearly crushed into the floor. "James!" Stella shrieked. But the weight was so... heavy...

He could hear fighting around him, but he could barely lift his own weight or open his eyes. Violet screamed... but then slithering, a cut-off hiss, and James could move again.

Stella helped him up. Ralph was fending off the remaining worms, and one charged Lucky only to be chopped in two by her Phoenix Sword. "Violet, hold on," Lucky said.

James looked, and saw Violet was slowing being covered by ice from the arm up. She breathed frantically, and Lucky stuffed the Phoenix Sword into her other arm. "Forget about me!" Violet said.

"No, we won't!" Ralph about shouted.

James knew they were probably doomed at that point. Stella grabbed him. "James, I need to tell---"

Whatever she was about to say was overwhelmed by another BOOOOM as another cannon exploded. The Eyedra HISSED, and grew even larger. 

Lucky stood up straight, walked back to the fire, pulled out her white bowl, and tossed a scroll in. It burned, she drew the circle, and said, with a steady voice _"Aruki A'aki Naraku Nalu."_

And then everything changed.
