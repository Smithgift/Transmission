## The Seventh Expedition

"Praise be to the ancestors... you're finally here." Then the Perfect looked at the three with confusion. "Where are the rest of you?"

"Sir," one of the men who had helped them. "There were only these three. Unless...?" He looked at them with hope.

"We're all ordinary Transmitted," James said.

"Surely you're joking," the lead Perfect said. "What are you talking about? We sent one of us down---we drew straws---to request reinforcements. Did the rest of you..." then horror spread across his face.

"There was no Eighth Expedition," Lucky said calmly. "I'm sorry. They discontinued the exploration a few years after the Seventh Expedition left. Some time after that, they started to Transmit criminals, and after that, everyone---"

"You can't be serious!" the lead Perfect cried.

"I am serious," Lucky said. "I've been tortured and worse by the Transmitted. These two came with me because they trusted me. There is no Eighth Expedition."

The lead Perfect fell to the ground and started to weep uncontrollably. "We waited so long..."

Another of the Perfects embraced him. "Father, be at peace. We have found new friends."

The third Perfect was a matronly woman, and immediately went to look over Lucky. Lucky, for once, did not shy away from touch. "Let me help you."

James had no idea what to say. For once he felt true pity for the Perfects---they must have been from the first few generations, before they had turned corrupt. And when the Tower of Worlds became a holding pen for criminals and the disfavored, soon enough they became totally isolated from everyone else.

"Would you mind letting us be undisturbed for a bit?" Stella asked the guard.

"Of course, ma'am," he said, and closed the door behind him.

Stella embraced James and kissed him hard. "I've been waiting for this for far too long." Then she looked up. "Please turn off your cameras for a bit."

"Uh..." James started.

"We _finally_ have our chance," she said with a gleam in her eye. "And I don't plan on waiting a second longer."

In response, James kissed her back.
