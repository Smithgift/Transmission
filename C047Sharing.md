## Sharing

James looked at Violet. "I'd love to hear it."

"The curse seems to trigger whenever only one person is fighting the monster," Violet explained. "And the curse says '(something) without (something) another (something).' What if it's 'fight a monster without another Transmitted?'"

They all looked to Lucky in confirmation. "Maybe," Lucky said, thoughts easily seen in her eyes. "I know some of those characters, but they weren't on the door. But at the same time, written Oru is obsessive about the sheer number of words for similar concepts. I'd have to check the door."

"Is it plausible, at least?" Stella asked.

"Very, because it seems like the weird sort of thing the Oru had hangups about. Sharing in everything, so maybe they didn't want anyone _not_ stealing another's kill." Lucky looked more thoughtful. "And the prohibited action did end with 'in nobility and _aretē_,' which is a phrase they use for morally unacceptable behavior.""

"What does 'fight' mean if it's that?" Ralph wondered. "We're all here... but we aren't wailing on the monster at the same time."

"We're supposed to take turns, maybe?" James asked. "I hit, then you hit, and back and forth?"

"That is going to be nurcking _impossible_ to coordinate." Ralph shook his head. "Maybe if we're careful. But the Oru don't seem to care about how hard things are."

"How about we go back and take another look at the door?" Stella asked. "We don't have any time limit, and maybe Lucky can read it better."

"Wait just one moment," Lucky said. She covered her face.

"Lucky?" James asked.

Skitters came from outside, and James readied his spear to see a yellow crab.

"That's the weakest they could send," Lucky said. "James, how about you try hitting a little by yourself? Don't kill it."

"Lucky, what---" Violet started, but James got close and kicked it twice. They all slammed into the ceiling. "James! Give us a warning!"

They crashed back down. "Ralph, let's alternate kicking it," James said, pushing himself back up. Ralph joined him, and together they battered the creature without issue.

"Good enough," Ralph said, as the crab futily scraped at their legs. "Any other test?"

"Try a mix of melee and missile?" Violet suggested.

James kicked it, and Lucky threw a dart. It disappeared into red then pieces. "Close enough," James said.

"Why did the Perfects send such a weak creature?" Stella asked.

"I asked my benefactors to send one," Lucky said, with a hint of a smug expression. "They have all the access to the cameras that the Perfects who hate us do."

"We know what to do now," James said. "Let's clear the rest of the dungeon, and be careful not to hammer anything we can't kill in one hit."

"All in favor?" Ralph asked.

They all raised their hands.

"Off we go, then."

* * *

Whether they fully understood the curse or not, James figured they had understood enough of it by how well things were going.

The fourteenth room held a three-headed ettin. "Careful!" shouted Lucky as James, Ralph and Stella advanced. James knew he was going to be careful---his head hurt from all the levitation. He stabbed it, narrowly avoided striking again. It swung at him, but Ralph chopped off its arm, leaving the stump glowing red. Stella got the kill with her sword.

"Don't get cocky," Ralph muttered. "We still don't know the exact wording of the curse."

On the way to clearing the rest of the dungeon, they ended up passing by their old marks and Lucky insisted on checking the entrance door again. "I want another look at the characters."

They stepped out, and James came face to face with Paul. "Oh, hey, James," he said. "Crazy seeing you down here."

James had to resist the urge to punch him or spear him. "Hey," he said, not bothering to keep the irritation out of his voice. "We're busy."

"Need another hand?"

"No," Ralph said firmly.

"Ah. I work here now, by the way." He glaced over to Lucky, who was staring at the symbols. "What the nurck is your magic Unwanted doing?"

James came very, very close to punching him. "Her name is Lucky," Stella said firmly. "And if you want to know, just ask her."

"So sue me for being curious," Paul said.

"Paul!" a voice bellowed from up the shaft. "Get working on the carts!"

"Got to go," Paul said, and went on.

The moment James thought he was out of earshot, he started "That creepy---" 

"---James!" Stella interrupted. "We don't know that this is anything! We didn't tell him we'd go dungeon crawling in here."

"Yeah, well, he was mean to Lucky," James insisted.

"I think Lucky is trying to concentrate," Violet said softly.

They all shut up.

"I have a _bit_ of an idea," Lucky said. "Let's go back inside."

"It's plausible. My best translation is 'fight (something) without (possible archaic word for honoring) another (some word meaning warrior.)' The standard words for Oru and monster weren't in there, so I assume it's only monsters filling a given role or a given time. And I did notice some radicals in the characters that seemed a little familiar, but Oru characters can be so complex it's non-obvious what the combination of radicals means. "

"Just assume it's all monsters and we'll be fine," James insisted.

"Let's be careful, though," Stella said. "We don't want to die from a mistake."

"Yeah, but there's no second curse... right?"

Lucky shook her head. "Whatever the Oru wanted this place for, they never desired anyone to not know what he was getting into by opening a door."

Ralph guffawed. "None of us do! 'cept you, Lucky."

A crab skittered down. James stabbed it, then on instinct moved to spear it again but stopped. The force of his lunge made him fall over, and the crab shoved a pincer into his face, hitting his eyes. "JAMES!" Stella screamed, but a whistle of a dart, and the pincer was gone.

"Nurck," James held his face, but felt Stella pry fingers off to rub a salve on his eyes. "I'm sorry, Stella. You're right. We need to be locked in."

"Just one more room and the Boss," Lucky said. "We can talk and joke after that."

No one argued with her.

* * *

The fifteenth room had been cleared after all, leaving just the final large door to the Boss. James was ready, though he knew one mistake could send them all flying.

Lucky tapped the door. "No trap. Let's go!"

They charged in. The next moment, Stella shrieked.
