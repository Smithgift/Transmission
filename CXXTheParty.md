## The Party

James stayed awake that night, thinking.




* * *

The Unwanted looked around with weary eyes, as if ground down over and over again.

"I'm Ralph ML, the Thinker's Lily Haskell, Fred Python's the tall guy, and Lucky is over there."

"I'm James Algol," James said. "I would have been a Salamander of the late Trog Tribe, but never got my Trickery high enough."

"Hah," Fred said, facepalming. "You're the first person to be honest about that."

"I like him already," Lily said.

"Where'd you get your equipment?" Ralph asked.

"We assaulted a Labyrinth and didn't make it," James said. "Only two of us survived, and then the other didn't make it back here."

"How many?" Lucky asked. Her voice was quiet.

"How many what?" James asked.

"Trogs."

"We were around four hundred."

"You shouldn't have attacked with so many," Lucky said.

"...What?"

"Lucky thinks that Guardian fights are scaled to the number of people in the Labyrinth," Lily said.

"I don't just _think_ it," Lucky muttered.

"I mean, she has a point," Ralph said. "Last time I worldclimbed, our Tribe was like fifty people. All Salamanders, though."

"You've worldclimbed?" James asked.

"All of us except Fred," Lily said. "I take it you haven't?"

"No," James said. "But I want to."

"See, Lucky's theory is that we can just assault a Labyrinth as a bunch of Wayfarers and make it, since we're going to be small enough that the bad stuff doesn't spawn," Lily said. "It's been done before. Met someone on the Surface who had been a Wayfarer."

Some part of James was about to argue, but realized before the words reached his mouth that that was just to defend his own Tribe. He needed to convince these people to take him. "I'm up for it," James said.

"Depends on what Lucky has to say," Ralph said.

"Show me your trees," Lucky said.

James pressed his nose with his thumb and showed them. His white tree was tall, and his black tree was medium-size.

"Good enough," Lucky said.

"What do you mean, 'good enough?'"

"Lucky has a knack for this kind of thing," Ralph said. "We don't question her knacks. But you want to join?"

"Yes."

"Then welcome to the party!"

The tension in his mind that had bothered him ever since the Dragon Trog Tribe was destroyed relaxed just a little. He had found a community of like-minded Transmitted. And with a little luck, they'd make it to the Surface.
