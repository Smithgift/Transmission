# Transmission

## The Trial

His trial was brief.

That morning, when he looked in the mirror, James Algol had known his chance of aquittal was so low that it had no meaningful reality. His mother remained insistent on him dressing his part. So he wore his suit over his still-thin frame, his Worker-dark hair perfectly combed, and his blue eyes still showing defiance. He could not fight them, but he would not give them satisfaction of seeing him afraid.

Yet when he stepped into the cramped grey courtroom, he didn't see them even eager to see his suffer. The small crowd of divine-class Perfects with their rainbow hair looked nothing but bored. This was nothing more than a tedious formality for them.

"You're late," the judge said, a white wig covering his Arbiter-white hair. Officially the Arbiters did not participate in the Scale, but they answered to the Perfects all the same.

"I arrived exactly on time," James said, taking his place at the defendant's stand.

"Silence. You are out of order."

James shut up. There would be an opportunity for him to speak, theoretically, but he could probably only say so much of the truth before he was forced to be silent. He would consider his words correctly.

The Perfect he had slapped stood up with a mixture of boredom and malicious pleasure shining from his white eyes, and took the witness's stand. "Your Honor. I was simply minding my own business one day..."

* * *

James had also been minding his own business. He and Stella were walking down one of the shafts as they usually did. Plenty of room to be anonymous, away from prying eyes and the watchers, when taking the hard way down.

Stella had a bonnet over her hair, which would normally have qualified her as an Unwanted, but that was the brilliance of it. No one would object to him having some "fun" with an Unwanted, when in truth she was a high-class Thinker. Had they been seen together as their true ranks, one or both of them would be Transmitted with little question.

They sat down near the edge of the steep pit, watching the lifts go by. "I wonder how long the emergency will last," James said.

"We have to keep working until it does," Stella said. "Which means it never will."

"I know, but---"

"Ssh," she said, and kissed him on the lips. He felt dizzy, and leaned in closer himself.

They could never marry, and they were always tempted to go all the way. But the chance that things would go wrong and---

"Oh, looky here," a voice with an audible sneer came from above. They looked up in shock to see a divine-class Perfect watching Stella with a leer. "I'll be taking her. You can have her afterwards."

"No," James said, standing up.

_"'No?'"_ the Perfect said, his voice containing simmering rage. He stepped down and grabbed Stella, wrapping his arm around her. She squirmed as he reached for her face with his own.

James slapped him.

"James!" Stella said, pushing the Perfect away.

"How---how dare you!" the Perfect shrieked, as if he had never even once felt pain before.

Stella pulled down her bonnet. The Perfect's eyes grew wide: he could do whatever he wanted with an Unwanted, but to lay hands on a Thinker would actually be considered a crime.

He smirked. "Well, well, I have quite the story to tell."

James seriously considered punching him. But he simply took Stella's hand. "We were busy. Let's keep being busy."

"Yes, we shall," she said, and followed him down the shaft.

* * *

The Perfect's version of the story was less full of lies than merely one continous lie. He left out Stella entirely, except that there had been a Unwanted involved.

"What have you to say?" the judge said.

"I don't know what you want to hear," James said. "The truth? The truth is that the Perfect tried to molest, or worse, a Thinker, and is now covering it up with a---" As he suspected, his words stopped coming out of his mouth.

Everyone knew that the Perfects, who could not lie, _did_ lie. But if anyone admitted that...

"I see," the judge said. He looked uncomfortable for a moment, but then finally said, "I sentence you to Transmission."
