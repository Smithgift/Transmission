## Survivors

It slammed shut.

Behind him.

The only other human outside was the older Thinker lady, who looked worse for the wear. So did James, he imagined. "You made it," he grunted.

"Barely. I think we're the only ones who escaped."

"Unless those inside killed it," James said.

The older lady shook her head. "I doubt it."

James also did, but didn't have the heart to do so. He opened up his stats screen to see his wound meter barely below the limit. He got out a salve and lay down.

"Let me help," the older lady said, helping him out of his armor.

"I'm James Algol," he said.

"Wanda D."

"How the nork did someone like you end up down here?"

"I was a librarian. The Perfects ordered a book destroyed and all references to its existence erased. I refused. So now I'm here."

Thankfully, James hadn't been so injured he fell unconscious, though that would probably be the end. The cream stung, and---

"We're the only survivors," James said.

"Yes, I realize that," Wanda said. "Could you help me next?

Wanda had been nearly clawed to death on top of luckily not being instantly killed by poison. James had to practically undress her entirely to treat her numerous glowing red wounds. He was glad that, whatever this place was, it had neither bodily fluids or gore.

At last they were dressed, re-armored, and sitting around a fire in the now empty camp. It felt so eerie, as if all the Trogs had been kidnapped in the blink of an eye. 

The outer doors had not budged, as if satisfied it had made its point about arrogant humans.

"Now what?" James asked.

"I was going to ask you that," Wanda said.

"We... we need to find somewhere. Another Trog Tribe, maybe even a Hold."

"Not a Hold," Wanda said. "I need to get up to the next world. My granddaughter was Transmitted."

"You're going to meet her in the Surface?"

"She said three was her lucky number, and we'd try to find each other in the third world, whatever it's called."

"I'm heading to two," James said. "My... girlfriend... and I decided to try to find each other there." James realized he had never referred to Stella as a girlfriend publicly, considering it would have gotten them both Transmitted. But now that they both had been, it didn't really matter, did it?

"Do you have any idea how to find another Trog Tribe?" Wanda asked.

"Nope," James said. "But I think we might be able to find our way back to Blue Sunrise."

"It's as good a plan as any."

* * *

Although it felt like trespassing, Wanda insisted on turning through all the goods the Dragon Trogdolyte Tribe had left behind now that it was left behind. Boris had been good, however, since by both of their reckoning they had the best equipment left. But they found a bunch of scrolls of unknown use---but scrolls nonetheless.

After that, James traced back the caverns. The two of them were by themselves, but they both had good equipment and good stats. Most of the time they spent in their own minds, thinking through what had happened and what went wrong. But ultimately, it was the Tribe's failure. Maybe if they had better equipment, better tactics...

Or maybe it was just impossible, or down to luck.

But Stella didn't believe in luck. James had never gotten a straight answer about what she believed was the case, but definitely not luck. If only---

"What got a nice young man like you stuck down here?" Wanda interrupted his thoughts.

James considered snapping back. But they had both seen some serious dung over the last few watches. "I loved a Thinker girl. We were meeting in secret, and a Perfect found out and wanted her. I slapped the nork out of him, and we both got Transmitted." Though, James realized, he didn't _know_ Stella was Transmitted.

The alternative was too grim to think about, ironically. "I suppose that's like me and---Contact!" Wanda cut herself off.

James whirled around to see a whole band of trolls charging out of a tunnel. James stood his ground, and slashed and cut and tried to dodge as they swarmed him.

But seconds later, they were all dead.

"Wanda, I---" he turned, then froze.

There was only a pile of equipment left where she had been.

James screamed with all his voice.
