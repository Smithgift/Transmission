## Interlude: Vulkan Lisp

Vulkan Lisp sat back in his chair, watching on his screen the party of the three people he hated the most went to bed. So Athena had finally done it. She had finally talked about the Oru.

Unfortunately, it didn't look like the two lovebirds were going to sleep with each other, and even more unfortunately, unless they were completely filled with their sick trans-Scale desires, they probably weren't going to do so while knowing he was watching. He swiped up the menu and sent them a leaf of _hamartia_ each.

How could that looselegs Thinker turn him down? He had offered---just one time, and she could stay. He'd even make sure there was no unwanting mingling of genes! But she had refused, calmly saying she would never do that. And then she had managed to find a Trog Tribe before he could even fully enjoy tormenting her with monsters.

That nurcking Worker, too, who had the _temerity_ to slap him. Every time he remembered it he became angry and angrier. He'd killed him 87 times with monsters, but that was still too few. Even when sending monsters in Labyrinths, or after that nurcker escaped that first time, he'd never managed to do it again. But that would change. Oh, yes, it would.

He looked through the menu again, and his finger hovered with his favorite words in Oru: _Send monsters._ But he knew that doing so would only be a confirmation of Athena's words. He hated her so much.

Why _hadn't_ she wanted him? Him, the son of a Supreme Council family! Their genetics would have produced a wonderful match, just as the Supreme Council had designed. Their children would have, in addition to being closer to pure-bred Oru, be in line for the Supreme Council themselves. But no. She had _wasted_ it. When they had stumbled on what she was doing in secret...

Some of the Perfects had it set up so that their favorite Transmitted always got topped up with _hamartia_ if it ever dipped too low. Vulkan never did that, preferring the delight of direct punishment over the efficiency of credits. Besides, he had more than enough credits to so exquistely spend.

But Athena had---she had not outsmarted him. No one ever could. But she was still too nurcking smart. He had stayed up all night so he _could_ send a Titan to crush her and the lovebirds, and they had managed to escape city limits just in time. And he didn't have unlimited credits... or did he?

He tapped the familiar time controls to rewind and clip the whole conversation. He didn't know why the Oru had built this place any more than his father did, but the Oru Perfects had evidentally enjoyed making their criminals suffer just as much as human Perfects. After all, the recordings of the cameras lasted for a whole year even without being clipped---and all of the good stuff had.

Vulkan tapped up an email. He would have to be respectful, but there was no reason Father would say "no."

> To: Achilles Lisp\
>
> Father,
>
> Despite our agreement with her prior to her Transmission, Athena has now told other Transmitted about the Oru. See attached clip for further information. I humbly request permission to use credits from the Stability Reserve Fund as needed to insure her talkativity does not spread.
>
> Sincerely, your son,\
> Vulkan Lisp

He tapped to attach the clip, then send it off.

It would take some time. Father would have to at least have an affirmative vote from another Supreme Council member to disburse the funds. But even a small fraction would be enough.

Until then, he had stayed up all night for no good reason so far. Time for some entertainment.

He swiped out of the live cameras and went to the archival footage, playing one of his favorites.

Vulkan Lisp had not been able to have Athena Perl. But he could have the next best thing...
