## The Auction

James watched in a very strange mix of satisfaction and distress at the clerk taking his old cert sheet and giving him a fresh one---one with "Level 1" clearly emblazoned under his name. Satisfaction, because he now could do far more. Distress, because he knew the more he got comfortable, the harder it would be to leave.

* * *

That same day Tim approached him. "Hey, since you're a Level One now, I found out I could take you to one of those special events."

"Sure," James said.






At that moment, he knew he could not remain at the Hold.
