### Arrow

"What the nurck is _that?_" James asked.

"That's..." Lucky took a deep breath. "That's impossible."

"What do you mean impossible?" Stella asked.

"That's an Oru arrow."

"A what?" James asked.

"An Oru arrow. You know, like the triangle at the end of a stick you draw to point at things. They used that shape."

"So the Oru are... but I thought they're dead."

"That's why this is _impossible,_" Lucky said. "We're beyond the reach of the cameras. I do know we had the ability to show arrows, but the Supreme Council decided to disable it. I don't know why. But that's what they look like."

The arrow shimmered, and then disappeared.

"So someone _can_ see us this far ahead?" Stella asked. "Maybe the Supreme Council decided to hide the fact you can go up to the fifth world?"

Lucky shook her head. "I mean, maybe. But if they did, wouldn't they rather kill us all? Send a monster we can't avoid."

"Here," James said, and opened up his destiny screen. "Whoever you are, if you're good guys, and you have our best interests in mind, make my _arete_ tree grow."

They all watched as a single silver leaf bloomed on his white tree.

"Impossible," Lucky said, her voice shaking. "Unless this is a trap..."

"_Could_ it be an Oru?" Stella asked. "Maybe there's some last colony somewhere that can control this world."

"Whatever they are, we might as well listen," James said.

"But... what if it's a trap?"

"Lucky, if we don't get you to some kind of healer, you might not stick with us. Especially if one of those machines comes rumbling around. I don't know if we have any other option."

"Agreed," Stella said. "Besides, if they wanted to neck us over, they could do so easily."

Lucky nodded. "Hey! Whoever's there. Show us that arrow again. We promise we'll follow it."

The arrow appeared again, and James and Stella helped Lucky up. "Off we go," James said.

* * *

The arrow appeared several times more on their trip, sometimes pointing off to the side of where they were previously going. James had time to think, and nothing he thought made any sense.

Who could possibly be contacting them? Why not earlier? Or was this all an elaborate scheme, as Lucky feared?

They helped her over one last dune, and James drew his weapon at the sight: a fortress, just like the other one. Had they been tricked?

A symbol glowed over the fortress. "Home?" Stella and Lucky said simultaneously.

Then another arrow, pointing at them, and another symbol. James could hear distant shouts. "What's that symbol?" James asked, now afraid.

"Help," Lucky said. "They're telling them to help."

Stella muttered under her breath. James squeezed her hand.

The figures came, wearing the strange Oru-clothing and Oru-armor that he had commonly seen in the Underneath. "Look! Over there!" One shouted in clear English, and they ran even faster towards the party.

"James, if we get separated---" Stella started.

"We come in peace!" one of the figure called, and James recognized he was even speaking in a regular Earth accent. They ran up the remainer of the dune and gathered around Lucky. "Here," he said, gasping for breath. Without hesitation he took out a salve and began rubbing it on her wounds. "Ma'am, do you feel pain on the inside? Do you need a pill?"

James could see the fear in Lucky's eyes---more strangers seeing she was a Perfect. But the strangers looked on her with awe and clear eagerness. "Pill would be good," she said.

Without hesitation the man---it was clearly so---pulled out a pill from his inventory and handed it to Lucky. She swallowed it and they helped her up.

"We've waited so long," one of the other men said. "Please, with us. It's dangerous out here."

James had no words, but followed the strange people back to their fortress.

* * *

The inside looked that mix of old and brand-new machinery that implied the humans had been here for a long time, but also had resources enough to maintain everything. Or at least James was absolutely convinced they were humans, even if they seemed obsessed with Oru culture. Banners with Oru writings hung from the walls.

One of the men with them belowed "They're here! They finally came!" Instantly every human in the place dropped his task and rushed to see them.

"They really came!" a Thinker woman cried. More noise followed.

"Please, ma'am, let us take you to the Supreme House," the man with them pleaded with Lucky.

Lucky, who had seemed increasingly bewildered and afraid, only nodded, and James and Stella followed her along.

The innermost structure was colored with rainbow banners, and looked more decorative than fortified. They hurried Lucky in. James followed, and then could not believe his eyes.

Three Perfects sat around a table, and they all got up and rushed to Lucky. "You're here!" one, a tall man said. "You're finally here."

Lucky looked at them with a mixture of confusion and fear. "Who... are you?" she asked.

"We're the Seventh Expedition," the lead Perfect said. "Or the remains of it."
