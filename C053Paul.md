## Paul

Paul looked a little worse for the wear---missing patches in his armor, and a sword tied awkwardly on his hip, as if he had had to buy a replacement in a hurry. He also had a hungry expression on his face.

"You nurcking stalker!" James shouted.

"Hey, whatever," Paul said. "You have magic. I want it, too."

"That's unfortunate," Lucky said icily.

"If it's a ring, I'll buy it off you," Paul said, not even looking at Lucky. "Or you can tell me how to craft my own. I saw it all: you can read the symbols on the walls, can't you? Even the scrolls? You even told the guard handing out materials you could read your own status effects."

James had to physically restrain himself from punching Paul. So he _had_ been stalking them after all, but maybe not even Stella. Lucky had been his target all along.

"Whether that's true or not, we don't have a reason to help you," Violet said firmly.

"Oh, come on!" Paul complained. "Give a nurck about your fellow Transmitted! Just have your magic Unwanted teach me how to use magic and I'll go."

"If you ask Lucky politely, maybe she'll agree," Stella said with a rare note of anger. "She is not anyone's property."

"Fine, fine," Paul said, rolling his eyes. He stared at Lucky. "Puh-leeze? I'll make it worth your while."

"No," Lucky said. "And that's final."

"Listen, you little---" He touched her bonnet.

James punched Paul enough to make him tumble over backwards. "Get the nurck out!"

"What the nurck, man?" Paul, said, rubbing the glowing red on his face.

"Everyone, please calm down," Stella said. "Lucky has the right to talk or not talk about what she knows as she pleases. Please respect that, Paul OCaml."

"Since when in nurcking history do Unwanted have rights?" Paul asked.

Ralph managed to grab James before he punched Paul again.

"Do you really believe the Perfects' nonsense?" Lucky asked. "That my rights depend on my hair color?"

Paul looked like he didn't even hear Lucky.

"You're not welcome here," Violet said.

"Too bad, because I'm not leaving until you give me magic. Besides..." He motioned all around him. "This is open terrain. You don't claim you own the wilderness, _right?_"

"I've been Transmitted a lot longer than you, kid," Ralph rumbled. "And I know that a party has a right to its own campfire."

"There's not a nurcking campfire in sight," Paul said.

_"Oru Haru Hakari Paru Pari."_ Lucky pointed at the tree, and a flame spear shot out and engulfed it instantly. She turned to Paul with a smug expression. "There is one now."

The greed in Paul's eyes only grew, along with a hungrier smile. "So that's it? The magic words? Oru Haru Hakuru---come on, help me out."

"Get your own campfire," James said. "This one's taken."

"Come on! It's not that difficult! Just teach me! It will cost you nothing!"

"Ignore him," Violet announced. "He'll go away once he learns he can't get anything from us."

"You _will!_ Teach me! Now!"

James knew this was going to be a _long_ night.

* * *

The first hour, or possibly several hours, Paul spent trying every argument to convince them. They had been friends back on Earth, right? And they all would benefit from worldclimbing. In fact, the whole of the Free Alliance would benefit if they had magic. Just think about all the slaves they could free? Slaves, you know. Don't you care about them?

Then he turned to trying to annoy them: shouting, singing off-key, whining, and calling them obscene names. Then he vanished.

Towards sundown he was back, circling them like a cat in the hydroponics impotently stalking the chickens behind the plexiglass. He made more arguments, then turned to outright pleading, begging forgiveness for his behavior. When that failed to elicit a reaction, he turned to screaming at them.

More than once, James considered teaching him a lesson with physical consequences, but Stella held his hand firmly down to the sand.

Unfortunately, they could talk nothing of import as long as Paul was in earshot, or if he was screaming. To James's regret, it did not seem like throats got scratchy in the Tower of Worlds, or if they did they healed at a minute cost to one's wound meter. And so on and on it went.

The sun finally dipped below the horizon. "Find your own campfire," Ralph said. "It's getting dark, and bad stuff can spawn out here."

"Nurck all of you," Paul said, and finally stomped off.

James waited until at least the form was distant enough. "That total dungpit."

"This isn't over," Violet said. "He'll be back tomorrow."

"I know it's not," James said. "But once we get to a city, he'll have to back off... right?"

"Could get a guard to intervene," Ralph said, stroking his chin. "But I reckon the bigger problem is if he tells everyone _else_ we have magic. Those green giants are going to be the talk of everywhere we go."

"Which means there's probably going to be a lot of imposters claiming they were the ones who summoned the giants," Lucky said. "At least it will give us _some_ cover."

"Lucky," Stella said. "Are you OK?"

James could now see what Stella saw: Lucky's face was far more exhausted than merely being harassed by that dungpit for hours. She shook her head. "Before I became Lucky, I tried telling some people. Big mistake." She took a deep breath. "One Hold... they tortured all the information they could out of me. Then when they got the information wrong and it messed up their enchantments, they killed me."

"Which hold?" Ralph asked, his voice harsh.

"Red Ivy." 

"I thought they collapsed?" Violet asked.

"Yes, having sky-high _hamartia_ on all their parties and constant monster attacks would do that," Lucky said, with sharp eagerness.

"Did hear some story about some Red-something Hold that had the worst possible luck," Ralph said, shaking his head. "Serves them right."

"It does," James said. "Sorry you had to go through that."

Lucky didn't reply, her eyes seeing something that wasn't there in the fire.

"Lucky, forgive me if this is a sensitive question, but do your benefactors... _listen_ to you if you'd ask?" Stella asked.

"They can. They don't always. And they don't always have the credits, I think. I don't have a way of hearing from them."

"Because I'm wondering if they're about to punish Paul with---"

_"HELP!"_ Paul screamed in the distance.
