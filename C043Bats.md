## Bats

James readied his spear, but the fighting raged outside the glass of the helm. Spells and arrows flew up and down, hitting mercenary or Slaver or just machinery. A crash rang out above the roof, and it started to cave in. They all ran outside to see the battle was going very badly for everyone involved.

Slavers tried jumping down with levitation scrolls, only to be torn apart by shadowy bats. Bats swooped down on the mercenaries, too, who had slightly better luck fending them off. Lucky took cover and started ripping scrolls, followed belatedly by James.

A bat shrieked down on them. Stella sliced into it with her sword but nearly fell off the hauler. James caught her, only for a second bat to crash into him and knock them both off.

The impact didn't hurt as much as he thought it would, although Stella cried out in pain. In the darkness, he couldn't tell who was who, but at least the bats were hostile to everyone. He stabbed one with his spear, only for it to not die and become a bat shiskabob. He hammered it against the side of the hauler until it died.

A party of Slavers came down on them---no military forks or spears, only swords. They got blasted from above by a series of fire spears. Most fell. The two remaining charged James and Stella. A bat bit through one's helmet, and Stella parried the others blow. James stabbed to find his spear ineffective, but someone jumped down and crashed him into the ground: Ralph. Together James and Ralph hammered at weak points until he flashed away.

A Slaver fell off the side of the canyon and died on impact. He had a glowing spear, which James grabbed. In the light, he could see bats everywhere, and people running, mercenary and Slaver alike.

Bats in a swarm swooped down towards James, only to be obliterated by a massive explosion of flame.

And then, the chaos faded. Aside from cries of pain and shouts, the battle had ended.

"Perfects," James said. "Perfects had to have seen all the Slavers nearby and sent something with all the total _hamartia._"

"We got lucky," Ralph said. "Shadow bats are extremely deadly. Fragile, but deadly."

"Where _is_ Lucky?" Stella asked.

Violet helped Lucky down the latter. Lucky's arm was a mass of red, and her face grimaced. "I can handle the wounds," she said. "Set something on fire."

James saw a pile of wood, and grapping his old enchanted spear, poked at it until it burned.

Lucky, with effort, opened up her inventory, and got a scroll to fall out. Violet threw it into the fire and it flashed blue. _"Oru hara haru aluri paru pari,"_ she chanted, and instantly the wounds disappeared.

"That was close," James said. Lucky nodded.

Elsewhere, mercenaries were tending to wounds, or finishing off injured Slavers. Stella looked around with concern. "Can't we help them?"

"My magic meter is almost full," Lucky said. "And you know we can't tell people."

"But why? It wouldn't reveal that you're a---you know---, just that you have magic."

"And they'll do things to me to make sure I keep using magic for them," Lucky said.

The captain of the guard came up to them, his whitemetal armor covered in scratches and scrapes. "We owe you one."

"No problem," Lucky said.

He opened up his inventory and pulled out a shining gem on a chain. "There must have been a Prince of Slavers here. I found this lying in a pile of high-end equipment. Take it---you saved us all."

Lucky took it with care. James almost asked for the armor instead, but Lucky's awe made him shut up.

Stella was also looking at it with happiness. "Thank you so much."

"You're welcome," he said, and walked on to the rest of the mercenaries.

"What is so awesome about that?" James started.

"This is worth more than everything in the caravan combined," Lucky said, holding the gem.
