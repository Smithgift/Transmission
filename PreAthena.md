

"How many flame spears do you use?" Ralph asked.

"A lot," Lucky said.

"Wait a minute," James asked. "Where were you keeping them?"

"What do you mean?" Lucky said, her voice rising a notch.

"Were all those scrolls in your inventory?"

"They're not in my inventory any more."

"Can I see your inventory?" James asked.

"What does it matter?" Lucky asked, her voice rising again. She started to tremble.

"You did agree to share your loot with the rest of us. That was the agreement," Ralph said.

"I have been," Lucky said. "Stop asking these questions!"

James opened his mouth, but saw the terror in Lucky's eyes, as if evil was about to occur, and occur to her.

Violet saw it, too. "It doesn't matter, does it? She's used those scrolls, and it killed a Guardian. We should just leave her alone."

"Yeah, I guess so," Ralph said. "Lucky, we do need to talk next time---"

"---it can wait until later," Lucky cut him off.

James felt uncomfortable. Everything had been going great, aside from Fred's death, and then this incident---which James had contributed to.

* * *

Lucky continued to act odd. She refused to talk to anyone, even Violet, and marched at some distance behind the rest of them. James thought about apologizing, but in truth, he didn't even know how he had hurt Lucky.

He probably should still apologize. He fell back a little. "Hey."

Lucky looked at him warily.

"Sorry for that incident."

She didn't respond for a few seconds, just marching on. "I forgive you," she said in a quiet voice.

James decided to march up ahead to rejoin the rest, just in case he stepped on Lucky's feelings again. Really, did it matter if Lucky was hoarding loot? It wasn't as if any of them would get any if it wasn't for her insane luck. And if she hadn't been hoarding all those scrolls...

Except, now that he thought of it, _where_ had she been hiding them? Scrolls would stack in the inventory, but had Lucky cleared her entire inventory of other items just to stock up on flame spears?

It didn't really make sense, either.

* * *

They ended up stopping in the forest when the sun started to set. James was glad there was still _something_ over his head.

The tension still hung over them, too, and James now wished he hadn't started that conversation at all. But there was no avoiding the next incident.

"Cut down _that_ tree," Lucky said, pointing to one that was taller.

Ralph shrugged, and started hacking at it with his axe. James, for lack of anything else, decided to patrol in search of brushwood. It was getting dark, though, so---"Ow!" His foot hit a log. "Hey, guys! There's a log already down here."

Ralph came over a moment later, and they eagerly hefted it up and brought to their camp. A moment from Ralph's knife and it was blazing. "That's easy," Violet said, as Lucky rejoined them.

"No it's _not!"_ Lucky suddenly erupted. "You need to cut down that tree!"

"Why?" Ralph asked.

"You just need to! That log won't work."

"It's just a log," James pleaded.

"It's the wrong _kind_ of log," Lucky said. "It's bad luck. Really bad lucky."

"Listen, Lucky," Ralph said with patience-restrained irritation. "I know you have hunches but I've been up here, and I've never heard a thing like this log thing. Why do they work differently in the Underneath?"

"Fine, then," Lucky said, looking around as if the world could end any minute. She reached up and tore off her Unwanted bonnet.

Beneath, in organized waves of color, was the hair of a Divine-class Perfect.
