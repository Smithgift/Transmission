## Eyedra

"Called it," Lucky said without mirth. In the distance, alarm bells rang out.

"Which one is it?" Stella asked, concerned.

"The Eyedra. Or at least that's what Transmitted call it. The Oru called it something along the lines of Eyeball of Worms."

"Less talking, more running," Ralph said. They drew their weapons and walked out, or tried to. Chaos ruled the inn outside the door as Transmitted shoved their way to the exit or tried to carry heavy chests. "On second thought..."

"It won't matter if we don't get---" Lucky started.

_BOOM!_ The floor rocked underneath them. Stella covered her ears.

"I hope that was one of our cannons firing back," Violet said.

"---We can't escape until it breaks the walls. So there's no use trying to get out right now." Lucky finished.

A loud HISS rang out, and the ground shook underneath them. The panicked Transmitted ran for it, dropping things in their wake. The party followed into the dark streets.

James looked around for the Eyedra and immediately knew he could never have spagetti with meatballs ever again.

The Eyedra towered over Ironspire and even shed its glowing light on the mesa behind it. Enormous eyeballs, the size of haulers, watched with indifference towards the humans it attacked. Hundreds of worm-like growths connected one eyeball to another, or had smaller eyeballs at the end. For one instant the night turned bright as day with a ray from one of the wall-cannons, slicing off a portion of the mass into red lines. It HISSED again, and the severed ends grew into new eyes like budding flowers, and more worms shot out to rejoin the rest of the Titan.

"Nurck," James said, as they all stood in the street watching. "Is it even killable?"

"A big city would have a chance," Ralph growled. "Not an outpost like this."

"It may look like it's regenerating, but it has a wound meter just like everything else," Lucky said. "Anyway, we'll try to escape as soon as it---"

The Eyedra pulled back one large eyeball and slammed into something, shooting debris far into the sky. Moments later, bits of stone rained on them.

"...breaks the walls," Lucky finished lamely.

"Let's move," Stella urged them. They headed towards the Eyedra. Soon others were following them, cheering war cries or shouting defiant taunts. The others thought they were going to fight, James realized, and wanted to join them.

More blasts of light fired from other cannons behind. The Eyedra HISSED again, and the wounds were soon only more eyes.

When they came close to the remains of the wall, now a pit in the ground, one massive eye watched them. Then the eye shot out worms the size of trees, which broke off and charged the Transmitted that dared approach their master. Lucky started chanting in Oru, while other Transmitted threw bolas or ripped scrolls.

No time to think! The worms were upon them. James stabbed his spear forward and it glowed as it pierced into a worm, killing it. Ralph hacked away at another worm flanking them, and Stella reached to protect Violet from a worm on their opposite flank. Then fire spear after fire spear struck worms, and they were incinerated.

More Transmitted fired upwards, striking eye or worm in the mass overhead. It HISSED and regrew, then a whole eye ripped itself off and rolled towards them.

Stella screamed. James almost turned---but some force within him held him in place, spear forward. The eye crashed into the front lines, glowing red wounds spouting everywhere, and turned as if to roll over. Lucky's Oru chanting turned to shouting, and fire spears slammed into the eye until it exploded.

Another wave of worms followed immediately after, save for one cycloptic worm staying a distance in the rear. It hissed, loudly enough to be understood. "Aru Aku Apari Alu Raui." A wave of firey rings cut through Transmitted, and the next moment, the lines broke.

"Retreat!" Ralph shouted, too late. They ran, but not in any organized formation. _BOOM! BOOM!_ Bright flashes came from behind. Stella tripped on a rock, and James dropped his spear to pick her up and keep running.

"Rally and hold!" a guard shouted, other Transmitted forming a disorganized mass around the professionals. James let go of Stella, and breathed. Then looked up. The Eyedra had only grown more eyes and worms, now turning to crush through Ironspire on the way to the next cannon.

"Lucky," Stella said, holding her as she shook.

"My fault..." she mumbled.

"Lucky?" James asked.

"That worm used magic," Violet said. "Does it have magic?"

"No," Lucky said, quiverring. "It... it used the color nearby." She looked at the ring on her finger in fear. "From me."
