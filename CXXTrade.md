

They travelled through larger and larger caverns, until they came to one that had a massive stone wall against one side. The tribe stopped, and someone blew a tune on a horn in a pattern.

They waited.

Another tune blew from the wall.

"They're letting us come close," A Trog told James. "We don't want to get them scared if we were to try to rush the walls."

"They're afraid of us?" James asked.

"Nah, more like it's courtesy. There hasn't been a Trog-Hold war in years."

James felt a concern he couldn't quite name.
